Studio

Green Art Studio was formally established in 2025, although we have worked successfully in this field for more than 20 years. We cover every phase of building design, interior design, furniture design and production, furnishing and construction, with professional supervision and advice.

We work to a high professional standard and aim to turn your needs and wishes into reality. Once permits are granted we also offer construction under design supervision, so that everything follows the details we have carefully developed. With selected, reliable contractors, your living space comes to life without much effort or stress on your part.

Olivera Jovanović

mr. spec. arch., founder and director

Graduate architect with a master’s degree in mechanical engineering and a specialisation in energy-efficient green architecture. After university, further study focused on self-sufficiency, passive building and bioclimatic architecture, through construction systems, natural materials, and heating and cooling from renewable sources.

We think green

Our speciality is bioclimatic green building to low-energy and passive standards. Alongside client taste and function, we aim to use natural, healthy materials that are harmless to people and the environment, and low-energy heating and cooling from renewable sources.

The race for energy savings and new artificial materials led to “sick building” syndrome, because many of them emit harmful electromagnetic radiation and fumes. That is why a return to nature, sustainability and natural materials is needed, along with education on the subject.

This concept considerably reduces running costs and raises the quality of everyday comfort.

Visual comfort
No radiators: everything is hidden in the floor, ceiling and walls.
Thermal comfort
The same temperature near the floor and the ceiling.
Air humidity
An optimal 55%.
